Unacceptable Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited in college campuses:

  • Racism: Discrimination or harassment based on race, caste, religion, or ethnicity.
  • Ragging: Any form of physical, verbal, or mental harassment of students.
  • Abusive Language: Use of insulting, threatening, or inappropriate words.
  • Bullying: Repeated behavior intended to harm or intimidate others.

If you experience or witness any such activity, please report it immediately.
